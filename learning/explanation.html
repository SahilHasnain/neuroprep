📋 complyDesk beta

Yeh app kya hai aur kis kaam aati hai — bilkul simple baat mein

Short mein: complyDesk ek shikayat / complaint tracker hai. Lekin yeh aam shikayat ke liye nahi hai — yeh company ke andar hone wali ghaltiyan track karne ke liye hai jo qanoon (law) se relacion rakhti hain.

💡 Example — real life story

Sochiye: Aap ek bank mein kaam karte hain. Ek customer ne apna Aadhaar card aur bank statement email kiya. Galti se woh email galat aadmi ko chala gaya.

Yeh data breach hai. Qanoon ke mutabiq aapko yeh record karna hoga, investigate karna hoga, aur deadline mein regulator ko report bhejni hogi.

Yahan complyDesk aati hai.

👥 Kaun kaun istemal karta hai?

🧑‍🎓

Compliance Officer 🎯

Woh insaan jiska kaam hai yeh dekhna ke company qanoon ke against to nahi ja rahi. Jab bhi koi ghalti hoti hai, woh complyDesk mein incident create karta hai aur track karta hai ke investigation kahan tak pahunchi.

🏦 Bank / Insurance / Hospital
🧑‍💻

IT Security Team 🛡️

Agar incident cyber hai (hacking, phishing, virus), to IT team investigate karti hai. Woh complyDesk mein timeline update karte hain — "yeh hua, woh hua, ab yeh kar rahe hain".

📱 Data breach / Hacking
🧑‍⚖️

Legal / Law Team 📜

Kuch incidents mein regulator ko report bhejni hoti hai (jaise RBI, SEBI, IT Ministry). Legal team complyDesk se PDF report generate karti hai aur regulator ko bhejti hai.

📄 GDPR / PDPB / IT Act
🧑‍💼

Manager / Boss 📊

Manager Dashboard dekhta hai — "aaj kitne incidents khule hain? Kitne band huye? Kaun si category mein zyada problem hai?" Bas ek nazar mein puri picture samajh aati hai.

💭 "Mujhe report do"

❓ Aam aadmi ko is se kya?

Directly kuch nahi. Lekin indirectly —

  • Aapka bank yeh app use karta hai → aapka paisa safe hai
  • Aapka hospital yeh use karta hai → aapka medical record safe hai
  • Aapka mobile company yeh use karti hai → aapka personal data safe hai

🎁 Yeh app logon ke data ki hifazat ke liye hai.

🏭 Kaun si companies istemal kar sakti hain?

🏦 Banks 🏥 Hospitals 📱 Telecom 🏀 Fintech 🏭 E-commerce 🏢 Insurance 📚 Schools / Uni 🏨 Govt departments

👉 Koi bhi company jo customer ka data rakhhti hai.
