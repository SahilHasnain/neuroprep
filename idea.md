# Pehle problem define karte hain

Tumhara customer kaun hai?

Ye sabse important question hai.

Possible users:

1. DPO (Data Protection Officer)
2. School Administrator
3. HR
4. Small Business Owner
5. Compliance Officer
6. Charity Organizations
7. Academy Trusts (UK)

Main **sirf ek** choose karunga.

> **Small Businesses (10-200 employees)**

Kyun?

* Inke paas dedicated compliance software nahi hota.
* Excel aur Word use karte hain.
* Budget hota hai.
* Pain real hota hai.

---

# Actual problem kya hai?

GDPR ka sabse bada issue documents nahi hai.

Issue hai:

> "Jab incident hota hai tab kya karna hai?"

Example

Employee ne galat email bhej di.

Ab owner sochta hai

* Kya ye breach hai?
* Report karna hai?
* Kitne ghante hain?
* Kisko notify kare?
* Evidence kahan save kare?

Ye panic situation hoti hai.
